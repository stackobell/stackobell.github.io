How I Work

Directly, and with a deliberately small client list

I take on a small number of clients at any one time. That's a constraint, not a sales line: it's the only way the person you hired stays the person doing the work. You deal with me from the first call through to delivery, and there is nobody to be handed off to.

Where a project needs a specialist I don't claim to be, whether that's development, video production, design or photography, I bring in people I've worked with for years and I manage them. You still have one point of contact, one invoice and one person accountable for the result. For bigger builds that's usually the better answer than stretching one person across everything.

Practically: I reply to enquiries within one working day. Audits are fixed scope and fixed price, with a date on them. Retainers run rolling monthly with 30 days' notice, because I'd rather earn the next month than lock you into twelve.

Scope

What I don't do

Worth saying plainly, because it saves us both a call.

I don't do SEO retainers, social media management or content calendars. I don't build websites from scratch. I don't take on brand strategy or creative campaign work. And I'm not the right fit if you're spending under [[ €1,000 ]] a month on ads, because at that level the fee eats the gain and you're better off running it yourself for now.

What I do is paid media, e-commerce conversion, and the tracking and reporting that tells you whether either is working.

If you're not sure whether your problem is one of mine, send it over anyway. If it isn't, I'll tell you and point you at someone better placed.

Tell me what's not working and what you're spending. I'll tell you honestly whether I can move the needle on it, and what that would cost.
