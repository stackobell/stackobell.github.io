Practical AI

The marketing and admin work that quietly eats your week, done in a fraction of the time. Half a day working out which jobs are genuinely worth handing over, set up properly on your own accounts. No retainer, no jargon, nothing to maintain.

The Problem

Most AI advice is written by people selling AI

You have been told this will transform your business. What nobody has told you is which three things in your actual week are worth changing, what it costs, or how to do any of it without putting your customer list somewhere it shouldn’t be. So nothing happens, and the sense that you’re falling behind gets a little worse each month.

The real wins for a small business aren’t dramatic. They’re the product descriptions that swallow a full day, the monthly report you rebuild from scratch every time, the enquiries that sit unanswered until Thursday because nobody had a spare twenty minutes. Boring, repetitive, and entirely fixable.

How It Works

Half a day, and then you own it

We go through how the work actually gets done in your business: what takes longest, what keeps getting put off, what you’d hand to someone else tomorrow if you could. Then we pick the handful of jobs where this genuinely helps and set them up together, on your accounts, using your real work rather than a demo. In person around Dublin, or over a call.

You finish with the tools configured, a plain-English page anyone on your team can follow, and a clear view of what isn’t worth bothering with. There is no retainer and nothing for me to maintain. If you want me back in six months when the tools have moved on, that’s a separate conversation and an easy one.
