Conversion Audits

Turn more of your existing store visitors into paying buyers. We audit your user journeys, identify hidden drop-off points, and implement high-impact fixes that lift conversion rates without increasing ad spend.

The Bottleneck

More traffic won't fix an inefficient checkout

When sales stall, the knee-jerk reaction for most businesses is to throw more money into paid ads. But driving traffic to an online storefront with friction in the checkout process simply burns your marketing budget.

Most online stores lose 70% or more of potential buyers right at the cart and checkout stages. Subtle issues-confusing navigation, slow mobile page loads, unoptimized payment options, or hidden delivery costs-kill transactions before they happen. Eliminating these barriers is the single fastest way to increase revenue and protect your gross profit.

Practical Delivery

Tangible, prioritised improvements-no massive rebuilds required

You don’t need an expensive six-month site overhaul to see significant uplifts. Most commercial gains come from targeted, high-leverage adjustments to key funnel steps.

I review your actual analytics data, session recordings, and checkout flows to identify exactly where your visitors are hesitating or abandoning. You receive a clear, plain-English roadmap of fixes: mobile layout optimisations, frictionless checkout setups, targeted cart-level upsells, and automated abandoned cart recovery sequences that recover lost margin right away.
