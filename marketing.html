Performance Marketing

Paid media engineered for net commercial return. We manage, test, and scale campaigns across Meta, Google, and paid search with ruthless focus on contribution margin—not vanity screenshots.

The Problem

You can’t take ROAS screenshots to the bank

Most agency reporting is designed to justify retainers rather than grow bottom-line profit. Reporting a "4x ROAS" inside an ad dashboard is meaningless if those conversions were branded searches you would have captured anyway, or if delivery costs, returns, and platform fees turn that revenue into a net loss.

Between signal loss, aggressive privacy updates, and rising CPMs across major ad networks, the old playbook of turning on broad ads and letting the algorithm do the work is over. Profitable growth requires clean server-side data infrastructure, creative testing frameworks that actually resonate with buyers, and managing ad spend against true Blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) and Cash Contribution.

Practical Answers

Common questions about paid acquisition

This is the most common trap in modern digital marketing. Ad platforms love taking credit for sales that were going to happen organically. If an agency runs heavy remarketing or bids heavily on your own brand name on Google, the platform dashboard will show massive ROAS numbers, but incremental revenue is close to zero. We strip out self-serving attribution and look at your Blended Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER)—total sales divided by total ad spend—to evaluate genuine business growth.

Ad costs have risen, but you don't need the biggest budget to win—you need the highest operational conversion efficiency. When your storefront converts at 3% instead of 1.5%, or your average basket value is €20 higher, you can profitably afford to acquire customers that your competitors cannot. Paid media should never operate in a silo; we integrate your ad creative directly with your on-site conversion funnel to maintain healthy unit economics.

It depends on intent. If people are actively searching for what you sell, Google Search and Shopping are your first port of call because the buying intent is immediate. If you need to generate demand or your product requires visual demonstration, Meta (Instagram/Facebook) remains unmatched in volume and scale. We don't dilute your budget across four channels simultaneously; we master the highest-converting channel first before expanding.

Relying on standard browser cookies is no longer enough. We implement first-party server-side tracking, Meta Conversions API (CAPI), and Google Tag Manager setups to feed clean, consented purchase signals back into the advertising algorithms. High-quality data inputs train the platform's AI models to find real buyers instead of low-intent clickers.

You don’t need enterprise budgets to test viability, but you do need enough spend to exit the learning phase and generate statistical significance. For most established SMEs, a focused spend of €1,500 to €3,000 per month on a single core channel is sufficient to validate creative angles, measure baseline CAC, and demonstrate whether the channel can scale profitably.
