SME Growth Strategy

Bridging the gap between digital activity and commercial performance. We align your online channels, operational workflows, and tech stack to drive sustainable top-line expansion and protect margin.

The Strategic Friction

Disconnected digital tactics don't equal a commercial strategy

Most SMEs don’t suffer from a shortage of ideas or tools; they suffer from fragmentation. Marketing runs ads in one corner, stock is managed manually across physical stores in another, and digital platforms operate as isolated silos disconnected from the core balance sheet.

Adding more software subscriptions or running reactive seasonal promotions won't break a revenue plateau. Sustainable scaling requires an overarching commercial blueprint: unifying online and physical sales channels, streamlining stock visibility, removing friction from customer journeys, and directing capital only towards initiatives that demonstrably move gross profit.

Practical Answers

Common strategic questions from business leaders

When a business hits a ceiling, the solution is rarely "do more of the same". We begin by breaking down your unit economics across channels: product margin distribution, customer acquisition costs, and repeat purchase behaviour. Usually, the bottleneck is structural—such as poor stock availability on high-margin lines, disjointed checkout experiences, or over-reliance on a single acquisition channel. We identify the primary constraint and build an execution plan to solve it.

True omni-channel isn't just having a website and a physical shop; it's creating operational harmony between them. This means accurate real-time inventory visibility across all locations, frictionless click-and-collect, unified customer data, and ensuring physical store teams view digital as an asset rather than internal competition. We build frameworks that turn multi-location physical footprints into a definitive competitive advantage over pure-play online retailers.

Agency retainers frequently outlast their initial utility. If you receive monthly reports loaded with vanity metrics like impressions and click-through rates, but struggle to see the net financial gain on your bank statement, the relationship needs commercial auditing. I act as an independent commercial advisor to review agency deliverables, evaluate technical proposals, and ensure external partners are held accountable to true commercial KPIs.
