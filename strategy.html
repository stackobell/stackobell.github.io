Service Area 03

Dashboards & Analytics

Turn fragmented metrics into commercial clarity. I build automated, single-view dashboards and deliver monthly commercial reviews so you always know where your revenue is coming from and what to do next.

The Friction

Drowning in data, but starved of commercial insights

Most business owners don’t have the time to log into four separate platforms every morning. Google Analytics 4 is needlessly confusing, Meta Ads shows one set of revenue figures, Google Ads claims another, and Shopify tells a completely different story.

When your numbers are scattered across different screens, making quick commercial decisions becomes guesswork. You don’t need 50-page PDF reports that arrive two weeks after the month has ended. You need one clean, real-time dashboard that tracks your true commercial health: blended ad spend, net revenue, acquisition costs, and margin.

The Retainer Model

A custom live dashboard paired with regular commercial recommendations

I build you a bespoke Looker Studio dashboard tailored to your exact business metrics, pulling live data directly from GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, and your e-commerce store into one clean, accessible view.

More importantly, the dashboard is paired with ongoing commercial advisory. Every month, I review the numbers, identify trends, flag underperforming campaigns or product lines, and provide clear, actionable recommendations. You get senior commercial direction on what to scale, what to cut, and where to focus your marketing budget next.
